SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Engineered for Academic Integrity

The portal eliminates ghost registrations, credential fraud, and unauthorized seat claims through an automated cryptographic workflow.

Deterministic Roll Verification

Only roll numbers pre-approved by the VIT Admissions Deanery can initiate an account creation. Format regex and whitelist checks execute instantly.

Atomic Single-Claim Lock

Every approved roll number can only be claimed once. Cloud database transactions prevent race conditions and duplicate onboarding attempts.

3D Holographic Digital ID

Registered students receive a dynamic 3D ID card equipped with encrypted QR codes for campus security gates, libraries, and examination centers.

Deanery Bulk CSV Intake

Admissions officers can ingest thousands of candidate roll numbers in seconds via automated CSV parsing with automatic validation and error reporting.

Role-Based Guarded Access

Strict separation between Student Dashboards and Admin Telemetry ensures sensitive student personal records remain completely confidential.

Multi-Campus Coordination

Centralized telemetry orchestrates admission slots, department rosters, and student identity across Vellore, Chennai, AP, and Bhopal campuses.
